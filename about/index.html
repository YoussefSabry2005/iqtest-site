About AI IQ Test

Challenge Your Brain. Discover Your Strengths.

AI IQ Test — Free Online Intelligence & Reasoning Test is a free, independent website for practising the kinds of reasoning puzzles found in aptitude and IQ-style tests: number and letter patterns, matrix reasoning, mental rotation, syllogisms and ordering puzzles. There is no sign-up, and your answers are scored in your own browser.

How questions are made and checked

Questions are generated from explicit rules, and each one is solved again by a separate program before it can be published. That solver sees only what you see and uses a wider set of rules than the generator, so any question where the key is wrong, or where a second option could also be defended, is thrown out automatically. New questions are published every day. Read the full details in how scoring works.

What your score means

Scores are shown with a confidence range because a short online test can never be exact. They compare you with other people who took this test, not with a representative sample of the population, and they are clearly marked as provisional until enough results have been collected. This is a practice and curiosity tool, not a clinical or professional IQ assessment.

Corrections

If you think a question is wrong or ambiguous, please tell us. We review every report and remove or fix questions that don't meet our standard.

Advertising

The site is free because it is supported by advertising. Ads are never shown inside the test itself, and advertisers have no influence over questions, scores or articles. See our privacy policy for details about advertising cookies.
