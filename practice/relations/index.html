Puzzle 1

The same rule turns each number on the left into the number on the right.

2 → 5, 5 → 11, 7 → 15, 6 → ?

A14
B12
C19
D7
E13
Show answer and explanation

Answer: E

Each number is doubled, then 1 is added. So 6 → 13.

Puzzle 3

6 → 16, 3 → 7, 2 → 4, 9 → ?

A34
B16
C25
D26
E24

Answer: C

Puzzle 4

The same rule turns each number on the left into the number on the right.

8 → 72, 6 → 42, 2 → 6, 3 → ?

A12
B11
C15
D13
E9
Show answer and explanation

Answer: A

Each number is multiplied by the next whole number (x × (x + 1)). So 3 → 12.

Puzzle 5

Answer: B
