Puzzle 1

What number comes next?

36, 47, 58, 69, 80, ?

A137
B91
C92
D182
E90
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B

Each number increases by 11. So the next number is 91.

Puzzle 2

2, 6, 8, 14, 22, ?

A38
B36
C72
D54
E30
Show answer and explanation

Answer: B

Each number is the sum of the two numbers before it. So the next number is 36.

Puzzle 3

What number comes next?

8, 45, 11, 51, 14, 57, ?

A60
B100
C7
D17
E15
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D

Two sequences are interleaved: the 1st, 3rd, 5th… numbers increase by 3, and the 2nd, 4th, 6th… increase by 6. So the next number is 17.
