Puzzle 1

Cleo is shorter than Hana.

Fay is shorter than Cleo.

Who is the second tallest?

BHana
CFay
DCleo

Answer: B

Chaining the comparisons gives the full order from tallest: Ava > Hana > Cleo > Fay. So the second tallest is Hana.
