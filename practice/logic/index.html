Puzzle 1

Assume both statements are true, and that each group has at least one member.

1. All drells are blips.

2. All blips are trels.

Which conclusion must be true?

ASome trels are not drells.
BSome drells are not trels.
CNone of these conclusions must be true.
DSome drells are trels.
EAll trels are drells.
Show answer and explanation

Answer: D

Combining the statements through the shared group "blips" guarantees: Some drells are trels. None of the other options is forced by the statements; each can be false in some situation where both statements hold.
