Logical deduction

Puzzle 1

Assume both statements are true, and that each group has at least one member.

1. Some fleps are trels.

2. Some quans are not fleps.

Which conclusion must be true?

ANo quans are trels.
BSome trels are quans.
CNo trels are quans.
DSome trels are not quans.
ENone of these conclusions must be true.
Show answer and explanation

Answer: E

The statements don't link trels and quans tightly enough: for every listed conclusion there is a situation where both statements are true but the conclusion is false.
