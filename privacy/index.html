What we collect

We do not ask for your name or email. Test answers are scored in your browser. To improve questions we receive anonymous, aggregated events: page views, which question IDs were answered and whether correctly, completion of a test, and errors. We don't use cookies for this and don't store IP addresses.

Advertising

We use third-party advertising, which may include Google AdSense. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visits. You can opt out of personalised advertising at Google Ads Settings or aboutads.info. Where required (e.g. in the EEA and UK), you will be asked for consent first.

Local storage

Your browser stores which questions you've seen (so tests stay fresh) and your last score. Clear your browser storage to remove it.
