Privacy policy

Last updated October 16, 2026

Who we are

AI IQ Test — Free Online Intelligence & Reasoning Test ("we") is an independent website offering free reasoning tests and puzzles. You can reach us through the contact page.

What we collect

We don't ask for your name, and you don't need an account. Test answers are scored in your browser. To keep questions accurate we receive anonymous, aggregated events: page views, the referring website, which question IDs were answered and whether correctly, completed tests and technical errors. We do not use cookies for this and do not store IP addresses or any identifier that follows you across days.

Contact messages

If you use the contact form, we receive your message and, if you choose to give it, your email address so we can reply. We keep messages for up to 12 months and use them only to respond and improve the site.

Advertising and cookies

We may show ads from third parties, including Google AdSense. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on visits to this site and/or other sites on the Internet. You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings or www.aboutads.info. Where the law requires it, such as in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, you will be asked for consent before advertising cookies are used. See also how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Local storage on your device

Your browser stores which questions you've already seen (so new tests stay fresh) and your last score. This never leaves your device; clearing your browser's site data removes it.

Children

This site is intended for adults and is not directed to children under 13 (under 16 in the EEA and UK). We don't knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR, Canada's PIPEDA or California's CCPA), you may have rights to access or delete personal information we hold, which for most visitors is none. To make a request, use the contact page.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
