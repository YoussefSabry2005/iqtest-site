How to solve ordering and ranking puzzles

Ordering puzzles give comparisons (“Ava is taller than Ben”) and ask about a position in the ranking. Writing a line is faster and more reliable than holding it in your head.

Rewrite everything in one direction

Turn every “shorter than” into “taller than” (Ben is shorter than Ava → Ava > Ben) so all facts point the same way.

Build a chain

Link facts that share a person: Ava > Ben and Ben > Cleo give Ava > Ben > Cleo.

Read off the answer

Once the chain is complete, count positions from the top. Ignore facts that are already implied by the chain; they are there as checks.

Try one

Puzzle 1

Dev is taller than Gus.

Fay is shorter than Gus.

Cleo is taller than Fay.

Cleo is taller than Dev.

Who is the second tallest?

AFay
BGus
CDev
DCleo
Show answer and explanation

Answer: C

Chaining the comparisons gives the full order from tallest: Cleo > Dev > Gus > Fay. So the second tallest is Dev.
